The Home

Eye level with the steeple.

Nearly 2,600 square feet on a single level, at the top corner of Rosewood. Tall French windows pull light from two directions over site-finished hardwoods, the great room gathers around a gas fireplace, and dinner comes together in a Wolf and Sub-Zero kitchen. A second set of French doors, under a transom, closes off a quiet bedroom wing with the primary suite at the far end.
